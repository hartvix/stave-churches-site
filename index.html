A little help along the way.

Support for Stave Churches, your companion for discovering churches, reading field guides and keeping a personal visit passport.

Do I need an account?

No. You can explore churches, read the available guides and record visits as a guest. Guest visits stay on that device until you sign in or remove them; they are not backed up while you remain a guest.

Why are my visits different after signing in?

On your first sign-in on a device, guest visits move automatically into the selected account. If you record more visits after signing out, the app asks whether to add them the next time you sign in; declining requires confirmation before those guest visits are discarded. If dates differ, choose which date to keep.

Signing out shows an empty guest passport. Your account visits and ratings should reappear when you sign back in to that account and the data is available; account changes waiting to be saved remain on this device for that account. Check the email in Account if you expected a different passport.

Apple and Google sign-in can share one app account when their verified email addresses match. Choosing a different sign-in method does not necessarily create a separate passport. If you chose Apple's Hide My Email, Account may show a private relay address.

Can I use the app offline?

The bundled guides and photographs are intended for offline reading. Account visit and rating changes are saved on the device and retried when connected. Sign-in, live community ratings and loading map data need a connection. Resolve any sync conflicts in Account before expecting changes to reach other devices.

Guest data and changes that have not synced can be lost if the app or its local data is removed. Do not remove the app to troubleshoot missing visits.

Why does the community rating differ from my rating?

When signed in, the visitor average excludes your own rating. Guests see the average of all eligible ratings. A rating contributes only while that account has a synced visit to the church. Removing a visit keeps its rating privately, so it can return if you mark the church visited again.

Is location access required?

No. You can enter visit dates without granting location permission. If permission is already enabled, the map can show your position and Log my visit can automatically record and verify today's visit when you are near an unvisited church. Otherwise, you can log a date manually and optionally verify it later.

Verification needs a fresh, sufficiently accurate reading within 200 metres of the church; the reading's reported accuracy is included in the check. You can change location permission in your device settings. The app does not track location in the background.

How do I delete my account?

While signed in, open Account and choose Delete my account. Read and confirm the destructive prompt. This requires a connection and permanently removes the app account, its synced visits and ratings, and that account's cached visits and ratings on the deleting device. After deletion is confirmed, guest visits on that device are also cleared.

For an Apple-linked app account, starting deletion also asks for fresh Apple confirmation. Cancelling that Apple sheet before a new deletion request is saved leaves the account intact. After confirmation, the backend verifies the Apple identity and revokes Apple tokens before deleting the app account.

Deletion removes the whole app account, including any linked sign-in methods. Your Apple and Google accounts are not deleted. Other devices can retain offline copies; they are not remotely erased. Signing in with the same Apple or Google identity after deletion can create a new, empty app account rather than recover deleted visits. Deleted records can remain in protected backups until they expire, normally within seven days.

If deletion is interrupted, use Retry account deletion. Keep the app installed and its local data intact. If asked to sign in again, use the same account and the sign-in provider requested by the app to finish deletion. You can use the separate guest passport while deletion needs attention. Contact support if you cannot regain access to finish the request.

Need current visitor information?

Check the church or museum's official website before travelling. Opening times, admission, services and accessibility can change. Respect local signs, private property and worshippers.
