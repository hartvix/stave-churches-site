These terms describe use of Stave Churches, published by Ole Kristian Hartvigsen. Questions can be sent through support. Our privacy policy explains how information is handled.

A guide, not a guarantee of access

The app provides discovery information, field guides and tools for recording personal visits. Historical interpretation and practical visitor information can change or contain errors. Check official church or museum information for current opening times, admission, services and accessibility.

The app is not an emergency service or a guarantee of safe routes or access. Follow local restrictions, respect private property and worshippers, and use your judgement when travelling. Optional location verification is a convenience for your passport, not an official certificate of attendance or a guaranteed position measurement.

Your account and records

Use an account you are entitled to access and do not interfere with other accounts, bypass access controls or manipulate community ratings. Record visits and ratings honestly. Do not send unlawful content or another person's private information to support.

Guest records and unsynced changes are device-local. Online services can be interrupted, and conflicting edits require your choice. Do not treat the app as the only copy of irreplaceable information. The support page explains guest visits, account saving and account deletion.
